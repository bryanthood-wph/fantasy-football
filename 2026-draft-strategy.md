## Variables checked at every pick (in order)
1. Tier drop — is the next player at this position materially worse than the one available now?
2. Gap to my next pick — odd rounds I wait 19 picks (5->25 style), even rounds I wait only 1 pick pair (36->45 is 9). Take the scarcer asset in odd rounds; the deeper position in even rounds.
3. Roster need vs. starters: 7 RB/WR-eligible slots. Target end state: 5 RB, 5 WR, 2 TE (or 1 TE + extra RB), 2 QB max, 1 K, 1 D/ST.
4. Bye stacking: cap 2 starters per bye week. AJB = 11. Watch bye 6 (Chase, ARSB, Jefferson, Achane, C. Brown, Higgins, Burrow, LaPorta) and bye 11 (AJB, Bijan, London, K. Williams, Judkins, Adams, Maye, Pitts, Fannin).
5. Q tag — hover before drafting. Q on board: Chase, CMC, Jeanty, J. Love, Hall, Nabers, Flowers, Egbuka, Odunze, Higgins, Swift, Mahomes, Warren, LaPorta, Kittle.
6. Positional runs — if 3+ of one position went in the last 6 picks, the next tier at that position is about to vanish; grab it or pivot to the position nobody is taking.
7. Queue 3 names before every pick so autopick can't hurt me if the clock runs out.
