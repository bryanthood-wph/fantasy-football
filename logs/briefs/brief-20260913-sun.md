# Week 1 Sunday brief — why each call (run 20260913-sun, v1.3)

Two changes, both locking at **1:00 PM ET, about four hours out** — inside the 12-hour rule, so make them in the ESPN app yourself. One thing is already settled: A.J. Brown played Wednesday in Seattle, caught 3 of 4 for 26 yards, banked roughly 4.1 points, and went on **injured reserve Friday** — minimum four games, back no earlier than Week 5. That slot is spent and locked. WR2 and FLEX2 are the live calls.

## 010 — WR2: start Jayden Reed, bench Rome Odunze

**Why Reed.** Thursday's run named the trigger in advance: if Friday tagged Odunze Questionable with no upgrade in participation, Reed starts. Friday tagged him Questionable with a right calf after a third straight Limited practice — Limited Wednesday, Limited Thursday, Limited Friday. The trigger fired exactly as written. Reed is not on Green Bay's injury report, neither is Jordan Love, and with Doubs gone to New England and Kraft being managed early, the short-area volume in Minnesota is his. ESPN prices Odunze at 10.8 a week against Reed's 8.3 — I am paying two and a half projected points for a receiver who cannot post a zero, and Reed kicks at 4:25, so the slot stays open three hours longer.

**Why not Odunze.** Rapoport reported this morning he is expected to go, and Odunze said Friday "I plan on playing" and called the calf muscle fatigue. That is real, and it is why this is close rather than obvious. Two things undercut it. Ben Johnson's Friday line was "we also got a long season coming up on us and we want to do what's right for him for the long-term" — that is how a coach pre-announces a snap count, and nobody has quantified one. And the premise Thursday leaned on, Odunze as Chicago's unambiguous WR1 after the D.J. Moore trade, does not survive contact: the Bears' own Week 1 preview features Luther Burden III and Colston Loveland and never mentions him. Both legs of the hold weakened at once.

**Why not Meyers.** He cleared the final report outright, better news than I expected. But the thumb dates to August 19, he was Limited all three days, and a thumb attacks catch rate directly. Jacksonville drew the only weather flag on the slate — 40 to 46 percent thunderstorms after 2 PM on a 1:00 kick — and Parker Washington, not Meyers, is the projected target leader in Liam Coen's offense.

**Why not Diggs.** Cleared — Dan Quinn said every man on the 53 is available. But he is 32, the No. 2 behind Terry McLaurin, and he drew Philadelphia's corner room, with Cooper DeJean in the slot where Diggs works. Availability is certain; production is the risk.

**What makes it wrong.** Odunze plays a full, unrestricted snap count while Minnesota controls the game and Reed sees eight touches.

## 011 — FLEX2: start Tony Pollard, bench Jonathon Brooks

**Why Pollard.** Thursday's case was that Brooks was hurt. That part is gone — Brooks was a full participant Friday, is off the injury report entirely, and Canales said "no limitations." The case survives anyway, because what was underneath it never depended on the groin. Pollard has no designation and has not appeared on Tennessee's report all week, is the uncontested lead back with the goal-line role, and Tyjae Spears is a receiving complement rather than a threat to the workload. The Jets are among the softest run matchups on the slate. ESPN separates the two by half a point a week, 10.0 to 9.4 — this is a role call, not a projection call.

**Why bench Brooks.** Chuba Hubbard is healthy, off the report, still the listed RB1, and Canales called the backfield "a tandem that we've been thinking about for a really long time," saying plainly that if Hubbard is good to go it is committee. Carolina is a home underdog with a 22-point implied total. A 1B in a two-man split is a bench body when a clean lead back is sitting there.

**What makes it wrong.** Tennessee's game total is 38.5, the lowest on the board, and their implied total is 20 — the safest role in the worst environment. Lose the script and Pollard's secure volume caps near 12 while Brooks catches a garbage-time touchdown.

## 012 — RB2: no change, Skattebo stays

**Why hold.** No designation, cleared from last October's ankle, listed starter, at home, 23-point implied total — the best environment of any back we own after Henry. **Why not Brooks here either.** Same committee, worse environment. **What makes it wrong.** That backfield is four deep: Tracy, Najee Harris and Singletary are all active, Tracy takes the two-minute work, and Graziano reported the Giants are "not all the way sold on the player."
