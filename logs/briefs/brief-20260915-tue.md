ESPN's 106 season / 6.3 per week was set before Sunday — stale low. He is the consensus #1 national add and CONTESTED here: T2 (waiver #1, RB 4/6, Jacobs on the exempt list) and T12 (owns McCaffrey) both have reasons to claim. At #10 we get him only if nobody does — GUESS 30%. A lost claim changes nothing.

FantasyPros already calls him "droppable with a chance of regret." He is the Wednesday-morning fallback if Black is taken.

**Why not Vele (the multi-hop).** The chain holds — only T9 has a WR slot open, so hot national WRs are uncontested here; Tyson's IR gave Vele 92% of Saints routes; Brown's IR thins our WR room — but it answers the wrong hole. Meyers, Diggs and Reed cover WR2/FLEX; the RB bench is Brooks at 19% of snaps, Henderson out, and a handcuff. Vele is a WR4 in Reed's tier. Optional Wednesday free add for Reed; not recommended.

**Why drop Brooks.** RB is 6/6, so an RB can only replace an RB. Brooks played 13 of 68 snaps behind a healthy Hubbard who scored twice. Henderson stays because he practiced Monday and is in play for Week 2; Hill stays because Henry is 32 on 24 carries a game and Hill took 46% of snaps Sunday.
