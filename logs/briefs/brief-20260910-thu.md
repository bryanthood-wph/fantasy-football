## 007 — Sunday FLEX: start Tony Pollard, bench Jonathon Brooks

**Why Pollard.** Brooks was Limited Wednesday *and* Thursday with a groin, and the back ahead of him got healthier this week, not hurt: Chuba Hubbard is off Carolina's report entirely, his camp hamstring resolved, and he is the listed lead back. Dave Canales' stated plan is "a lead back for the series, the other guy comes in on third down" — Brooks is the other guy, and he is the one with the soft-tissue injury. Pollard does not appear on Tennessee's Week 1 report at all; the only two Titans on it are Keldric Faulk (shoulder, Full both days) and Cedric Gray (concussion, DNP both days). He is the uncontested lead back with Tyjae Spears behind him, at home against the Jets, chasing a fifth straight 1,000-yard season. Starting a healthy every-down back over an injured timeshare back is the whole argument.

**Why not Justice Hill.** The new name on the roster, and the temptation is to see what we bought — but his job has not changed: Baltimore's No. 2 behind a fully healthy Derrick Henry, also off the report, whose new coordinator said Thursday he is getting "a pretty large workload." Hill is about four points a week standalone. His value is what happens if Henry misses time, and that has not happened.

**Why not Reed or Meyers in this slot.** Both are FLEX-worthy on talent. Reed is better held as the clean answer to the Odunze question below, and Meyers is Limited both days with a jammed thumb and practiced Thursday in a non-contact jersey — a thumb attacks a receiver's catch rate directly.

**Why bench Brooks.** Groin, limited twice, second in a committee behind a healthy starter, on the road. Canales is "very confident" he plays — but playing is not producing, and his likely share is about a third of the backfield.

**What makes it wrong.** Hubbard's hamstring flares in Friday's practice and Brooks inherits a real workload.

## 008 — WR2 contingency: hold Odunze, with Reed named as the replacement

**Why hold Odunze.** Limited Wednesday and Thursday with a right calf — a new issue, which Ben Johnson explicitly called "something different" from last year's foot — and Thursday was his first real participation since it happened. Johnson: "He's more day-to-day right now… but we are hopeful he will be ready to go on Sunday." He is Chicago's unambiguous WR1 after the D.J. Moore trade, at home against Carolina. GUESS 85% he plays. Two days out that is a start, not a bench.

**Why Reed is the named replacement.** He is off Green Bay's report entirely, so is Jordan Love, and Reed is the slot centerpiece on a new extension. No designation, no ambiguity, a defined role.

**Why not Meyers.** Same thumb problem, plus his own Questionable risk — swapping one injury question for another is not a hedge.

**Why not Diggs.** Genuinely healthy and off Washington's report, but 32, the No. 2 behind Terry McLaurin, and he drew Philadelphia. Third name, not second.

**What makes it wrong.** Odunze is tagged Questionable Friday, plays a full snap count anyway, and we benched a WR1 for a slot receiver. Friday's designations resolve it; Sunday's run makes the final call.

## 009 — A.J. Brown: hold, no free-agent add before Sunday

**Why nothing before Sunday.** Brown's Week 1 is already spent — 3 catches on 4 targets for 26 yards before a Seattle corner landed on his right ankle in the third quarter Wednesday. X-rays negative, diagnosis high-ankle sprain, Thursday's MRI sets the timeline. Vrabel: "I don't know if he'll be available for the next week… Continue to evaluate. Continue to run tests." The hole is Week 2, not this weekend, and nothing done tonight changes a single Sunday point.

**Why not add Ridley or Jeudy now and drop Diggs.** Because this pool does not run away. We are WR 6/6 and so is every rival but one — a WR add costs them a WR drop too, which is why no WR in this league is ever contested. Calvin Ridley (24% rostered), Jerry Jeudy (28%) and Denzel Boston (26%) will still be there Tuesday, when we will know the MRI grade and Friday's designations. Spending the spot tonight means guessing Brown's timeline on the worst information we will ever have.

**What makes it wrong.** The MRI reads Grade 3, Brown lands on IR, and the one rival with an open WR slot takes the best name Friday.
