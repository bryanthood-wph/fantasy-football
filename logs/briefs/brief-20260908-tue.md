## 004 — Waiver: claim Allgeier / drop Jones (selected: safe). Colby chose Hill / drop Jones.

**Why Allgeier.** He is the only free-agent RB with a starting job right now: James Conner is on IR (min 4 games) and rookie Jeremiyah Love is "50-50" with a high-ankle sprain, the kind that lingers. That makes him worth ~7–9 pts/wk for two to four weeks, which matters this week because our RB depth is thin — Henderson is OUT Wednesday and Brooks is questionable in a 1A/1B with Hubbard. He is the one player in the pool other managers actually want (36% rostered; all nine rivals have an RB slot open; T2 holds waiver #1 and just lost Josh Jacobs to the exempt list), so he is the only place claim priority buys anything. A lost claim costs nothing.

**Why not Hill (the multi-hop).** Not because he is worse — because nobody else wants him. No other team in this league rosters Derrick Henry, so no one has handcuff motive; Hill would still be there Wednesday morning as a free add. Claiming him spends priority on something you could have for free. His value is contingent: ~4 pts/wk standalone, RB1 on a top-5 offense if Henry (32, 2,000+ carries) misses time — GUESS 18% in the next four weeks. Plan was: claim Allgeier, and if it fails, free-add Hill for the same slot. You skipped straight to Hill, which is the same end state if the Allgeier claim would have lost (GUESS 55% T2 takes him at #1).

**Why not Boston (the exciting).** Wrong position for this week's hole — the RB room is thin, the WR room is fine — and dropping Diggs removes the WR half of the only legal trade shape. Also a WR is free any morning in this league: only T9 has a WR slot open, so no WR is ever contested. Nothing to decide tonight.

**Why drop Jones.** RB is 6/6, so an RB can only replace an RB. Jones is 31, third body in Minnesota's committee, with a snap count capped by design. Dropping him gives no bye-6 relief (he is bench; the bye-6 starters are Williams + Bates either way).

**What makes it wrong.** Love plays a full workload Sunday. ARI's report drops Wednesday, after the clear, so this could not be resolved before the deadline.

## 005 — D/ST: free-add Titans / drop Eagles (selected: safe). Colby did it.

**Why Titans.** This league scores D/ST only on touchdowns, safeties and 2-pt returns. ESPN's projection (Eagles 107, Titans 68) measures sacks, INTs and points allowed — none of which score here. Counting 2025 six-point plays instead: Patriots 6, Titans 5, Browns 4, Jets 4, Eagles 1. Tennessee keeps Chimere Dike (2 punt-return TDs) as PR1 and KR1, hosts the Jets with a 35-year-old QB on a new team, and sits on bye 9 where we start nobody — which frees bye 10 (Odunze + Eagles were at the cap) for a WR in the trade lane. 6.5% rostered, so no claim needed.

**Why not Patriots (the exciting, and the best unit by 2025 count).** Our own bye rule kills it: Patriots, A.J. Brown and Henderson are all bye 11. Pre-flight passes today only because Henderson is hurt and modeled as bench; the day he starts it is 3 starters on one bye, cap 2. They also play tonight, so they had to be added and started before 8:20 PM, and 68.8% national ownership made a rival claim possible.

**Why not Jets (the multi-hop).** Same game as the Titans, one fewer 2025 six-point play (4 vs 5), and bye 13 lands on Henry's bye and hits the cap while bye 9 is empty. A tiebreak, not a quality gap — they are fallback #1 if the Titans ever disappear.

**What makes it wrong.** Dike loses the return job.

## 006 — Trade: Pollard + Diggs to T2 for Waddle (selected: safe). Colby declined.

**Why this shape.** We are RB 6/6 and WR 6/6, so any WR coming in needs a WR going out; Pollard-for-a-WR straight up is illegal (WR 7/6). Every 2-for-1 puts the partner at 17 and forces a cut, so the question is who has the cheapest cut and the sharpest need. T2's cut is Cyrus Allen (42 proj, the cheapest player rostered in the league) and their need is the sharpest: Jacobs on the exempt list indefinitely, Jeanty limited, Corum a backup, two WRs starting at FLEX. Pollard is Tennessee's RB1 — a real starter, not a throw-in — which is why he can buy a starting WR. Waddle (27, DEN WR2 with Nix) would start at FLEX2 over Brooks (+14 season projection) and Diggs (32, our WR6) becomes part of the price instead of a future drop. Legal only after the Titans swap (bye 10: Odunze + Waddle = 2).

**Why not McMillan (the exciting).** Better player, ~15% acceptance: T9's RB need vanished when MarShawn Lloyd became Green Bay's starter, and McMillan is their WR2 behind a questionable Nacua.

**Why not Burden (the multi-hop).** The chain is real — Charbonnet out, Monangai questionable, T4 at waiver #10 so they cannot win an RB Wednesday, we are the only RB-capped roster — but T4 fields a legal lineup without an RB3, their forced cut is a real player (Charbonnet or Shakir), and they carry Burden as their highest-projected FLEX option. GUESS 30% vs T2's 40%.

**Why propose anything from 9th of 10 by projection.** A lateral move is pointless; this one is consolidation (+14 at a starting slot, paid with a bench RB and a WR6). Declining is fine — the cost is that Diggs stays a 32-year-old WR6 with no exit and FLEX2 stays a Brooks/Hubbard coin flip. Pollard as RB insurance while Henderson is out is the honest case for keeping him.
